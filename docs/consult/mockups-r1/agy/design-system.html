Academic Research Identity

Warm paper. Crisp ink. Unwavering provenance.

The Saurus is not another SaaS dashboard. It is a digital reading desk tailored for scholarly literature synthesis. In light mode, it evokes archival research papers; in dark mode, aged parchment under soft candlelight.

Core Axiom: Synthesis, not summary Traceability: Every claim has an address Transparency: You can watch it think Agency: The researcher stays the author

Token Section 03

Citation & Receipt Anatomy

"A chatbot gives you an answer. The Saurus gives you the receipts." Citations are first-class interactive instruments pinpointed to paper, page, and paragraph.

1. Inline Address Syntax

Citations are composed of an index anchor in scholarly brackets followed by a monospace coordinate tag specifying page and paragraph offset.

"...demonstrated sustained expression in mice [1](p.12,§3)."
  • Bracket Index: Numeric reference mapping to corpus bibliography.
  • Coordinate Suffix: (p.12,§3) — Verified exact location in the original PDF source.
  • Hover Interaction: Unveils verbatim claim sentence and NLI entailment score.
  • Click Action: Opens PDF viewer overlay pre-scrolled to highlighted paragraph.

2. Active Receipt Card (Hovercard)

"AAV9 achieved 60% hepatocyte transduction efficiency at 1×10¹² vg/kg in adult C57BL/6 mice without detectable hepatotoxicity."

Token Section 06

Scientific Callouts & Discrepancy Alerts

When research papers conflict or methodology limits claims, The Saurus highlights it rather than glossing over it.

Methodological Disagreement Detected · Off-Target Profiling

Rodriguez & Singh [5] (p.15,§4) observed detectable off-target insertions via unbiased genome-wide GUIDE-seq, whereas Okafor et al. [4] (p.9,§2) reported no detectable mutations. The discrepancy is attributable to detection assay sensitivity: [4] screened only the top 8 computationally predicted loci, while [5] surveyed genome-wide double-strand breaks.
