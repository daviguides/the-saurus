# The Saurus — Variant B: Processing State

## Target: Nano Banana

## Context
This is the mascot for the PIPELINE RUNNING state — the dinosaur is actively digesting papers. Cheeks full, concentrating, working hard.

---

## Prompt

A baby brontosaurus with its mouth full of papers, cheeks puffed out, eyes squinting with concentration. Actively chewing/processing.

### The Dinosaur
- Same baby brontosaurus from the approved hero illustration — IDENTICAL character design
- Baby proportions: oversized round head, big expressive eyes, curved neck, compact rounded body, short stubby legs, short upturned tail
- 2-3 small rounded dorsal bumps along spine
- Monocle on visible eye (thin gold circular frame, chain draping down neck)
- Facing LEFT

### Expression
- Mouth CLOSED and FULL — cheeks visibly puffed/bulging with paper content
- A small paper corner or edge sticking out from the corner of the closed mouth — couldn't quite fit it all in
- Eyes squinting with effort — concentrating hard on chewing
- Slight frown of concentration — not upset, just focused
- The vibe: a hamster with stuffed cheeks, trying to chew. Comically focused.

### Pose
- Standing firmly, slightly hunched — all energy going to "digestion"
- Body leaning slightly forward
- Small motion lines around the jaw/cheeks suggesting active chewing

### Processing Indicators
- 2-3 tiny sparkles or small stars near the head — suggesting thinking/processing (NOT a thought bubble, just small decorative marks)
- Optional: a small open notebook or quill on the ground beside the dinosaur, partially written — the review is being drafted

### What's NOT present
- NO flying papers (already eaten)
- NO finished review book (not done yet)
- NO labels, baselines, or diagram elements
- NO background scene

### Style
- Hand-drawn ink line art, single stroke weight, dark academic green (#2D6A4F)
- Gold (#D4AF37) only on monocle frame and sparkles
- NO fill on body — line art only
- Must match the hero illustration style exactly

### Format
- Square (1:1), 1024x1024 or 2048x2048
- PNG with transparent background
- Character centered, occupying ~60% of frame
- Must work at 48px (sidebar icon) and 400px (pipeline view)

### What to AVOID
- Do NOT show an open mouth — mouth is FULL and CLOSED (stuffed cheeks)
- Do NOT make it look sick or uncomfortable — it's focused, not suffering
- Do NOT add thought/speech bubbles
- Do NOT change the character design
- Do NOT make it face right — faces LEFT
