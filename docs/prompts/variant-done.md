# The Saurus — Variant C: Done State

## Target: Nano Banana

## Context
This is the mascot for the REVIEW COMPLETE state — the dinosaur has finished digesting all papers and is proudly presenting the finished review. Satisfied, belly slightly rounder.

---

## Prompt

A baby brontosaurus with a satisfied closed-eye smile, proudly presenting a finished "Review" book. All papers consumed, job done.

### The Dinosaur
- Same baby brontosaurus from the approved hero illustration — IDENTICAL character design
- Baby proportions: oversized round head, big expressive eyes (closed in this variant), curved neck, compact rounded body, short stubby legs, short upturned tail
- 2-3 small rounded dorsal bumps along spine
- Monocle on visible eye (resting on closed eye, thin gold circular frame, chain draping down neck)
- Facing LEFT
- Belly SLIGHTLY rounder than idle state — visually suggesting it ate well (subtle, not exaggerated)

### Expression
- Eyes CLOSED with a gentle satisfied smile — the "mission accomplished" face
- Mouth closed, slight upward curve at corners
- Head held high — proud posture
- The vibe: a cat that just finished a meal and is extremely pleased with itself

### Pose
- Standing tall, chest slightly puffed
- One front leg extended forward, presenting/offering the Review book
- The book rests against or near the extended leg — being shown to the viewer

### The Review Book
- Bound book with dark green (#2D6A4F) cover
- "Review" written on cover in cream/light text
- Gold (#D4AF37) bookmark ribbon hanging from bottom
- Small gold checkmark or star floating just above the book — verification/completion signal
- 2-3 small sparkle/radiance lines around the book — freshly produced, precious

### What's NOT present
- NO papers (all consumed)
- NO diagram elements, labels, or baselines
- NO background scene

### Style
- Hand-drawn ink line art, single stroke weight, dark academic green (#2D6A4F)
- Gold (#D4AF37) on: monocle frame, bookmark ribbon, checkmark/star, sparkles
- The Review book is the ONE element with a color fill (dark green cover) — everything else is line art
- Must match the hero illustration style exactly

### Format
- Square (1:1), 1024x1024 or 2048x2048
- PNG with transparent background
- Character centered, occupying ~60% of frame
- Must work at 48px (sidebar icon) and 400px (completion view)

### What to AVOID
- Do NOT show open eyes — eyes are CLOSED (satisfied)
- Do NOT show any papers — they're all eaten
- Do NOT place the book near the tail/rear — present it FORWARD, near the head/front leg
- Do NOT make the belly grotesquely fat — just slightly rounder than idle, subtle
- Do NOT add speech bubbles or text on the dinosaur
- Do NOT change the character design
- Do NOT make it face right — faces LEFT
