# The Saurus Mascot — Image Generation Prompt

## Target: Nano Banana (or equivalent image generation tool)

---

## Main Prompt

A hand-drawn mascot illustration of a friendly dinosaur (stegosaurus-like) eating scientific papers. The dinosaur is a product mascot for an academic tool called "The Saurus."

### Composition
- The dinosaur faces LEFT, in profile view
- Mouth is open, actively chomping on 2-3 PDF pages that are halfway into the mouth — papers sticking out at angles, some crumpled slightly from being bitten
- One small arm reaches up holding another paper toward the mouth
- The body is round and slightly chubby — friendly, not fierce
- Short dorsal plates along the spine (3-5 small triangular plates, not sharp)
- Thick tail curves down behind, resting on the ground
- Two sturdy short legs, standing firmly
- Near the tail or on the ground beside the dinosaur: a finished bound book/notebook labeled "Review" with a gold bookmark ribbon — representing the output

### Expression and Personality
- Eyes are round, slightly oversized for comedic effect — one visible eye looking at the papers with hungry enthusiasm
- Small monocle on the visible eye (thin gold circle with a chain) — the academic touch
- Slightly smug, satisfied expression — like a scholar who just found the perfect paper
- The tone is COMIC but not cartoonish — think scientific illustration with personality
- Playful but not childish — a mascot for researchers, not children

### Style
- Hand-drawn ink illustration style, like a field notebook sketch
- Single-weight line art — consistent stroke width throughout (~2px equivalent)
- Minimal detail — no shading, no gradients, no fills except the papers and book
- Lines should feel slightly organic/imperfect — not vector-perfect, more like a confident pen drawing
- Color: monochrome dark green (#2D6A4F) lines on off-white/cream background (#FAFAF7)
- Papers: light gray with faint horizontal lines suggesting text
- "Review" book: dark green fill (#2D6A4F) with cream text and a gold (#D4AF37) bookmark ribbon
- Gold (#D4AF37) accent on the monocle frame only

### What to AVOID
- Do NOT make the dinosaur look realistic or threatening
- Do NOT make it look like a lizard or gecko — it should be clearly a dinosaur (stegosaurus body shape)
- Do NOT make the body muscular or "bombado" — it should be round, soft, friendly
- Do NOT place the review/book coming out of the tail end (this looks like defecation)
- Do NOT make it face right — must face LEFT
- Do NOT add a background scene — isolated character on transparent or cream background
- Do NOT use thick outlines or cartoon bubble style
- Do NOT add color fills to the dinosaur body — line art only, interior is empty/background color

### Size and Format
- Square composition (1:1 aspect ratio)
- High resolution (at least 2048x2048px)
- PNG with transparent background preferred
- The dinosaur should occupy about 70% of the frame, centered slightly left
- Leave breathing room around the character

### Reference Mood
- Think: Moleskine notebook doodle by a biology PhD student
- Think: The kind of drawing a professor would sketch on a whiteboard to explain a concept
- Think: Friendly, scholarly, slightly self-aware humor
- NOT: children's book illustration
- NOT: corporate mascot
- NOT: pixel art or digital art aesthetic

## Variant Prompts

### Variant A: Idle State (empty state, waiting for papers)
Same dinosaur, mouth closed, looking expectantly to the left with a slightly tilted head. No papers. Monocle on. One arm extended palm-up as if waiting to receive something. "Review" book not present — nothing has been produced yet. Caption mood: "Feed me."

### Variant B: Processing State (pipeline running)
Same dinosaur, mouth full of papers (cheeks puffed out slightly), eyes squinting with concentration. Small motion lines around the jaw suggesting chewing. Tiny stars or sparkles near the head suggesting thinking/processing. "Review" book partially visible, being written — open, with a pen/quill resting on it.

### Variant C: Done State (review complete)
Same dinosaur, mouth closed, satisfied closed-eye smile. Belly slightly rounder than idle state. One arm presenting the finished "Review" book proudly, held up and open. A small checkmark or gold star floating above the book. No papers visible — all consumed.

## Usage Context
This mascot appears in:
- Landing page hero section
- App empty state ("Feed The Saurus your papers")
- App loading/processing states
- Error states (variant: confused expression)
- Documentation headers
- Social media / Open Graph images

The mascot should work at sizes from 48px (sidebar icon) to 800px (landing hero). At small sizes, the monocle and papers are the key recognizable elements.
