# The Saurus — Logo Icon Prompt

## Target: Nano Banana

---

## Prompt

A minimal logo icon showing ONLY the head and neck of a friendly dinosaur, in profile, facing RIGHT.

### Anatomy
- Long curved neck rising from bottom-left, ending in a small rounded head at top-right
- Head is small relative to neck — NOT a T-Rex with a huge skull. Think brontosaurus/sauropod proportions: long elegant neck, small head
- Mouth slightly open with a hint of a smile — friendly, not menacing
- One visible round eye, slightly oversized for personality
- Small monocle on the eye (thin circular frame with a short chain hanging down the neck) — the signature academic detail
- 1-2 tiny dorsal bumps/plates visible where neck meets the cropped edge (hint that there's a body below)
- A single small paper/page corner sticking out of the mouth — subtle, like it just grabbed a snack. NOT a full page, just a triangular corner with one faint text line

### Shape Language
- The neck curve is the dominant shape — a graceful S-curve or C-curve, like a question mark without the dot
- The silhouette should be instantly recognizable at 16px (favicon) and 200px (header)
- The negative space inside the neck curve is part of the design — keep it open

### Style
- Hand-drawn ink line art, single consistent stroke weight
- Monochrome dark academic green (#2D6A4F)
- NO fill on the dinosaur — just the outline/stroke
- Gold (#D4AF37) ONLY on the monocle frame
- Lines slightly organic — not vector-perfect, like a confident single-pass pen drawing
- Minimal — as few strokes as possible while remaining recognizable

### Usage as Logo
- This icon sits to the LEFT of the text "The Saurus" in Literata serif
- At brand sizes (header, ~40px height): icon + text side by side
- At favicon size (16-32px): icon only, monocle and paper corner may not be visible — the neck curve alone must read as "dinosaur"
- The icon should feel like it belongs next to a serif typeface — scholarly, not playful/bouncy

### What to AVOID
- Do NOT include the full body — CUT at the base of the neck (or fade/crop naturally)
- Do NOT make the head large or T-Rex-like — small head, long neck is the character
- Do NOT face LEFT — logo faces RIGHT (opposite of the hero illustration, so they face each other in layout)
- Do NOT add details that disappear at small sizes — keep it to: neck curve, head shape, eye, monocle, paper corner
- Do NOT use thick cartoon outlines
- Do NOT add a background shape (circle, square, shield) — the silhouette IS the logo
- Do NOT make it look like a snake or seahorse — the dorsal bumps/plates are the dinosaur signal

### Format
- Square canvas (1:1), icon centered
- SVG preferred (for web), also PNG at 512x512 and 1024x1024
- Transparent background
- The neck should use approximately 60-70% of the vertical space
