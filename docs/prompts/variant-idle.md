# The Saurus — Variant A: Idle State

## Target: Nano Banana

## Context
This is the mascot for the app's EMPTY STATE — when no papers have been uploaded yet. The dinosaur is waiting, hungry, ready to be fed.

---

## Prompt

A baby brontosaurus standing alone, looking expectantly to the LEFT with a slightly tilted head. Waiting to be fed papers.

### The Dinosaur
- Same baby brontosaurus from the approved hero illustration — IDENTICAL character design
- Baby proportions: oversized round head, big expressive eyes, curved neck, compact rounded body, short stubby legs, short upturned tail
- 2-3 small rounded dorsal bumps along spine
- Monocle on visible eye (thin gold circular frame, chain draping down neck)
- Facing LEFT, head tilted slightly upward — the "waiting" pose

### Expression
- Mouth CLOSED (no papers to eat yet)
- Eyes wide open, looking expectantly — slightly upward and to the left, as if watching for incoming papers
- One subtle eyebrow raised — curious, hopeful
- The vibe: a puppy waiting by an empty food bowl. Eager but patient.

### Pose
- Standing on all fours, weight slightly forward (leaning toward where papers would come from)
- One front foot slightly lifted — about to step forward
- Tail gently curved up — alert, not drooping

### What's NOT present
- NO papers — the dinosaur has nothing to eat
- NO review book — nothing has been produced
- NO labels, baselines, or diagram elements — this is the character alone
- NO background scene — isolated on transparent/cream

### Style
- Hand-drawn ink line art, single stroke weight, dark academic green (#2D6A4F)
- Gold (#D4AF37) only on monocle frame
- NO fill on body — line art only
- Slightly organic lines, confident pen drawing
- Must match the hero illustration style exactly — same character, different pose

### Format
- Square (1:1), 1024x1024 or 2048x2048
- PNG with transparent background
- Character centered, occupying ~60% of frame
- Must work at 48px (sidebar) and 400px (empty state)

### What to AVOID
- Do NOT add papers or books — this is the EMPTY state
- Do NOT make it sad or dejected — it's eager, not depressed
- Do NOT change the character design from the hero illustration
- Do NOT make it face right — faces LEFT
- Do NOT add speech bubbles or text
